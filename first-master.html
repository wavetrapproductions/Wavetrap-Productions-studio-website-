Before you send

Just starting out

Keep it simple

  • Song up to 3 minutes
  • Export as WAV, not MP3 or WhatsApp audio
  • Nothing on the master channel: no limiter or "loudness" plugin
  • The meter never hits red; loudest part around -6
  • Name it Artist - Song - Mix v1.wav

Not sure? Send it anyway. We check every file and tell you in plain words.
